Peng Kuang 邝鹏

NEX lab, Dept. of Computer Science, Lund University, Sweden

The spiky EEG signals on the screen behind me show the muscle artifact caused by my smile :)

I’m a WASP-affiliated Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Lund University, advised by A/Prof. Emma Söderberg, Prof. Martin Höst, and Dr. Diederick C. Niehorster. My research lies at the intersection of programming systems (languages and tools), eye tracking, and human-centered machine learning, with influences from design science, HCI, and cognitive neuroscience. Additionaly, I’m interested in multimodal intelligence (for education and health), brain-machine interface, and programming systems on emerging computing platforms such as VR/XR.

I did my Master of Information Technology at The University of Melbourne where I worked as a RA for Prof. Shanton Chang. My master thesis was supervised by Prof. Leon Sterling and A/Prof. Antonette Mendoza. Before that, I was studying at Monash University. I was awarded the International Merit Scholarship by the Falcuty of Information Technology and received my Graduate Certificate in IT there. I have an industrial background in the gaming industry and a bachelor in Literature.
